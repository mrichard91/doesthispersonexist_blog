[Michael Ligh](http://www.mnin.org/) just released the latest in the Anatomy of a Phish series title "[Anatomy of a Phish III](http://www.opensecnet.com/phish_3.pdf)."  
  
As always we had a lot of fun researching and dissecting the tools and techniques used by the bad guys in their never ending quest for a debit card, CVV2 and PIN. Like all great script kiddie stories it's not complete until the bad guy leaves some incriminating evidence via IRC (Idiots Really Chat).  
  
Don't forget to check out "[Anatomy of a Phish II](http://www.mnin.org/write/2005_phish_2.pdf)" and "[Anatomy of a Phish I](http://www.mnin.org/write/2005_phish.pdf)".
